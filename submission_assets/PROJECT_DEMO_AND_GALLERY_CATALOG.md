### Spoken Narration & Storyboard Segments

1. **00:00 – 00:20 | The Problem & The Instant Hook**
   - *Narration:* "Every year, cross-border merchants lose billions when network timeouts strike checkout. An HTTP 504 gateway timeout occurs: the customer assumes the payment failed and clicks pay again, causing catastrophic double billing. PayBridge AI resolves this ambiguity with mathematical truth before duplicate charges occur."
2. **00:20 – 00:40 | Architecture & Core Invariants**
   - *Narration:* "Unlike fragile black-box automation, PayBridge AI is built on strict financial invariants. Our golden rule: AI never moves money. Autonomous agents analyze evidence and produce formal claims, but only authenticated human operators can authorize mutations through protected, single-attempt cryptographic locks."
3. **00:40 – 01:00 | Executive Dashboard & 3D State Vector**
   - *Narration:* "Here in the enterprise dashboard, operations teams monitor active payment cases in real time. Notice our three-dimensional state vector: tracking PayPal gateway ground truth, internal application state, and recipient bank settlement simultaneously to expose discrepancies the moment they emerge."
4. **01:00 – 01:20 | Golden Path Demo B — PayPal Checkout & 504 Timeout**
   - *Narration:* "Let's launch Golden Path Demo B. We initiate a genuine PayPal sandbox order. As capture processes, we simulate a severe upstream network drop. Instead of crashing or letting the buyer blindly retry, PayBridge instantly locks the checkout session with DO NOT REPEAT CHECKOUT."
5. **01:20 – 01:40 | Autonomous Multi-Agent DAG (A01–A10)**
   - *Narration:* "Behind the scenes, our multi-agent DAG investigates autonomously. Agent A01 fetches PayPal ground-truth capture telemetry, while Agent A03 verifies signed webhook signatures. Every agent's analytical claim is strictly validated against an immutable SHA-256 evidence vault before entering the state reducer."
6. **01:40 – 02:00 | Interactive Truth Gap Graph**
   - *Narration:* "The Truth Gap Graph provides instant visual clarity. Here we see the precise asymmetry: PayPal successfully captured the funds, but the confirmation response was dropped in transit. The engine classifies this as Gap Zero-One: a confirmed capture requiring immediate customer reassurance."
7. **02:00 – 02:20 | Two-Lens Symmetry (Sender & Receiver Portals)**
   - *Narration:* "PayBridge delivers coordinated two-lens transparency. The buyer's portal clearly explains that their payment succeeded and instructs them not to retry. Simultaneously, the merchant portal confirms receipt, keeping both parties in complete lockstep without contradictory customer support tickets."
8. **02:20 – 02:40 | Cryptographic Payment Truth Passport**
   - *Narration:* "When truth is reached, PayBridge issues an immutable Payment Truth Passport. Backed by SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and signed evidence references, this passport serves as undeniable legal and financial proof of payment for merchants, customers, banks, and auditors alike."
9. **02:40 – 02:57 | Iris AI Financial Copilot & Closing Impact**
   - *Narration:* "With conversational intelligence powered by our private sub-second SLM, operations teams get instant natural-language truth. PayBridge AI turns chaotic payment timeouts into verified financial certainty. One payment, two parties, one immutable truth. Thank you."
